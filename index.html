Your Ultimate Guide to Shopping on Temu: Tips, Tricks, and Hacks

4 min read Updated Today

If you’ve been on the internet at all recently, you’ve probably seen ads for Temu. With its ultra-low prices and massive variety of items—ranging from quirky kitchen gadgets to affordable fashion—it’s easy to see why it has become incredibly popular.

But if you are new to the platform, navigating the endless scroll of deals can be a little overwhelming. Here is your normal, no-nonsense guide to getting the most out of Temu.

1 What Exactly is Temu?

Temu (pronounced tee-moo) is a massive online marketplace that connects consumers directly with millions of sellers, manufacturers, and brands globally. Because you are buying directly from the source, the prices are deeply discounted compared to traditional retail.

2 3 Tips for Shopping Smart

When prices are this low, it is important to shop strategically to ensure you actually like what arrives at your door.

  • Always read the reviews: Don't just look at the star rating. Scroll down and read the written reviews, specifically looking for comments about material quality and durability.
  • Check the customer photos: This is the most important step! Seller photos can be heavily edited. Customer upload photos will show you what the item actually looks like in real life and in normal lighting.
  • Pay close attention to sizing: Sizing can vary wildly between different sellers on the app. Always check the exact measurements provided in the description and compare them to your own measurements, rather than just clicking your usual "Medium" or "Large."

3 Maximizing Your Savings

Temu is already cheap, but they gamify the shopping experience to give you even more discounts. Here is how to take advantage of them:

Play Games

Spin the wheels and play quick games when prompted to yield real coupons.

Lightning Deals

Check these time-sensitive discounts on popular items for extra savings.

Leave in Cart

Leave items in your cart to sometimes trigger extra discount notifications.

4 Shipping and Returns

Important Note:

The biggest trade-off for Temu's low prices is the shipping speed.

Expect your order to take anywhere from 1 to 3 weeks to arrive, as many items ship internationally. Temu does offer a tracking feature in the app so you can watch your package's journey.

If you don't like an item, Temu generally has a very generous return policy. They usually offer free returns within 90 days for your first return on any order. Just print the label provided in the app and drop it off at the designated shipping carrier.
